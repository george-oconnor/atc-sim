Reading the log

onstartthe recogniser is running
onaudiostartit can hear something, before it knows if it is speech
onspeechstartit thinks it is hearing speech
interim / FINALa guess at what you said, then its settled answer
onerrorsomething went wrong — the code after it says what
onendthe recogniser stopped (and, if the test is still running, it is restarted automatically)

If the level meter moves but no onresult line ever appears, your microphone works but the browser cannot reach its speech-to-text service — try a different network, turn off a VPN or ad blocker, or try a different browser.
